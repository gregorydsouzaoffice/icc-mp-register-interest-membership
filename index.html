About ICC

The Indian Chamber of Commerce, founded by Shri GD Birla in Kolkata in the year 1925, has in recent years been scaling across the globe as India’s National Chamber of Commerce. Having already established offices in 15 states within India and 25 foreign countries, ICC is now setting up its Madhya Pradesh Chapter to actively initiate its operations in the state.
